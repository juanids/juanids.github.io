  <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 300px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.77408" target="_blank">Nanowire Nanocomposite Networks with Controlled Resistive Switching and Learning Abilities (2026)</a></h3>
    <p style="text-align: justify;">In this work, we leveraged theoretical model predictions to develop a novel nanocomposite material that enables enhanced reproducibility and control over the resistive switching of nanowire networks. We analyzed its dependence on environmental humidity and successfully mitigated these dependencies through an effective encapsulation strategy. Furthermore, we performed the first in-materia processing tests on this material based on associative learning, observing behaviors highly compatible with reservoir computing architectures.</p>

    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202410766" target="_blank">Two-junction model in different percolation regimes of silver nanowires networks (2024)</a></h3>
    <p style="text-align: justify;">We investigated the diverse electrical behaviors exhibited by silver nanowire networks across different percolation regimes. By proposing a simple two-junction model alongside numerical simulations, we successfully explained the various observed transport dynamics. Additionally, this publication provides direct experimental evidence—via SEM imaging—demonstrating that nanowire burning occurs during the electrofusing process.</p>

    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/aelm.202200631" target="_blank">Resistive switching of self-assembled silver nanowire networks governed by environmental conditions (2022)</a></h3>
    <p style="text-align: justify;">This research presents the first demonstration of electrofusing in these networks, leading to subsequent resistive switching. We observed a strong dependence on environmental moisture—while ensuring the samples were not electrically stimulated under high humidity conditions—and provided a comprehensive physical explanation for the underlying mechanisms driving this behavior.</p>

    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1088/2058-8585/ac97a5" target="_blank">Multipurpose nanocomposite resist for free-standing transparent conductive thin films (2022)</a></h3>
    <p style="text-align: justify;">We developed a novel material based on a PMMA matrix embedded with silver nanowire networks to form free-standing films. These versatile films can be deposited onto various surfaces and even stacked, successfully maintaining high electrical conductance while preserving excellent optical transmittance.</p>
